# Lisa - AI Content Distribution & Repurposing Platform

> **Create once. Adapt intelligently. Publish everywhere possible. Learn from performance.**

Lisa is an AI-powered multi-tenant SaaS content operations platform that transforms a single original piece of content into platform-optimized, publishable variants across multiple channels (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest, Email, and Blog CMS) with an analytics-driven feedback loop.

## 🚀 Core Capabilities

- **Brand Intelligence System:** Maintain consistent tone, guidelines, forbidden phrases, audience personas, and RAG-based knowledge retrieval.
- **Specialized AI Agents:** 10 discrete agent roles (Intake, Strategy, Adaptation, Caption, Media, QA, Analytics, Recommendations, etc.) orchestrated with deterministic validation.
- **Media Derivative Pipeline:** Automated smart cropping, resizing, transcoding, safe-zone validation, and thumbnail generation.
- **Content Calendar & Approval UI:** Human-in-the-loop review, inline editing, batch approval, and drag-and-drop scheduling.
- **Reliable Publishing Engine:** Multi-mode publishing (Direct API, Draft upload, Manual handoff) with idempotency controls and bounded backoff retries.
- **Analytics & Learning Loop:** Normalized cross-platform metric collection and evidence-backed future content recommendations.
